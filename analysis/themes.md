### Own the verification loop

**Trust in agent-generated systems comes from owning the verification loop—deterministic rules, stakes-scaled credentials, replayable execution traces, hardened sandboxes, and continuous failure diagnosis—rather than line-by-line diff review.**

As generation drops to minutes, human diff review becomes the bottleneck and fails silently against compounding slop or benchmark gaming. Teams maintain quality at scale by converting corrections into code-enforced lint rules and invariants, gating tools by risk via Token Vault scopes and CIBA approvals, running agents in isolated VMs with video/trace artifacts, and closing an immune-system production loop that catches, diagnoses, remembers, and regression-tests failures—including cross-checking uncalibrated LLM judges in shadow mode.

### System One decision models

**Sub-second, reliably calibrated decision models turn AI from heavy conversational chatbots into a programmatic fourth logic gate—running inside DuckDB queries, UI keystrokes, and live web-grounded pipelines.**
