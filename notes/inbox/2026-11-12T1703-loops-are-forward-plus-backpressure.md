Loops = forward pressure + backpressure.

In Dexter Horthy's "12-Factor-Factories: Design Patterns for Agentic Coding" (HumanLayer; 1325061, Mainstage). His answer to the question in 2026-11-12T1702-forward-and-backward-pressure.md: a loop is the combination of pressure pushing work forward and pressure pushing back on it (checks, failures, limits). Connects to Justin McCarthy's "loop" mode (2026-11-12T1527-loop-then-continuous.md) and the "Close the loop" theme.
