Being ambitious != doing more.

During Ishaan Dey's "Judgement is the Last Job" (1376195, Mainstage). A qualification of the "Ambition is the limit" theme (analysis/themes.md): Simon Willison and Cognition's Walden Yan say ambition is now the constraint, while Scott Tolinski's survey found developers feel pressure to produce more simply because AI makes it possible. This note separates the two: ambition is about what you aim at, not volume.
