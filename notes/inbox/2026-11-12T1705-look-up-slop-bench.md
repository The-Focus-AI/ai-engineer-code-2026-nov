In Dexter Horthy's "12-Factor-Factories: Design Patterns for Agentic Coding" (HumanLayer; 1325061, Mainstage).

Looked up 2026-11-12 ~17:06 (web search; not verified by the talk-links node): likely SlopCodeBench, a benchmark from UW-Madison (Gabriel Orlanski and colleagues; arXiv 2603.24755, March 2026; NeurIPS 2026 poster) that tests whether coding agents degrade as they keep extending their own code over many checkpoints. No agent fully solves any problem end to end; the best checkpoint pass rate is in the mid-teens (sources give 14.8% or 17.2%, versions differ). Dex Horthy (HumanLayer) has run Claude models through it; aggregator coverage reports Opus 5 passing 4 of 17 checkpoints with quality warnings on most output, but his own post wasn't found. A September 2026 piece says HumanLayer archived a large codebase after agents degraded it.
