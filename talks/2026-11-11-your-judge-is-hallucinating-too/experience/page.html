The check

Run a second check in shadow mode

Auditing your evaluator does not require pausing production or swapping out your stack upfront. Run a second check alongside the primary judge in shadow mode and record where the two scores part ways:

“Run a second check. Run in shadow mode, seeing where it disagrees.”00:00:00

Closing rule

Start small and read the disagreements

Skip designing an elaborate benchmark suite before looking at real diffs. Start with a small slice, pull up the cases where the two checks disagreed, and read them directly—because the failures hiding inside a trusted judge rarely look like what you expect:

“Then read the cases, see where the two disagree, and don't overthink it. Just start small.”00:00:00
