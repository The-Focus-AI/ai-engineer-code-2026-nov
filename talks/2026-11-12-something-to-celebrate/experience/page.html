2027 roadmap

Consistent flagships and global partnerships

Rather than endlessly adding flagship conferences, swyx notes the team scaled from one flagship event a year to three last year and four this year—and expects to hold roughly steady next year (perhaps adding a smaller fifth) while scaling geographically through local partnerships:

“We started with one event a year. Last year, with three events a year. This year is the fourth. Flagship events of the year. Next year we're probably going to stay roughly consistent. Maybe add a small. Fifth, one.”00:04:17

Following the first China partner edition in Shanghai the week prior, he highlights newly announced AI Engineer India (Bangalore, Q1 2027) and points prospective local organizers to the online partnership form before sending attendees to the after-party:

“A lot of you have asked us how to run an AIE in your city. And well, just apply online. You know, there's there's a partnership form.”00:04:17
