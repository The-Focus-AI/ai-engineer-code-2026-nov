## Four flagships a year—and AI Engineer goes global

Closing out (`00:04:17`), swyx maps how the event series itself scaled: from one flagship event a year, to three last year, to four flagship events this year (`00:04:17`). Rather than endlessly multiplying flagships, next year holds steady around four (perhaps adding one smaller fifth event) while growing through local city partnerships (`00:04:17`).

> “We started with one event a year. Last year, with three events a year. This year is the fourth. Flagship events of the year. Next year we're probably going to stay roughly consistent. Maybe add a small. Fifth, one.”
> — 00:04:17

Following a partner event in Shanghai the week before—the first in China—he highlights newly announced **AI Engineer India** (`ai.engineer/india`, Q1 2027) and invites organizers interested in hosting a local edition to apply through the partnership form (`00:04:17`), before sending the room off to the after-party (`00:04:17`).

> “A lot of you have asked us how to run an AIE in your city. And well, just apply online. You know, there's there's a partnership form.”
> — 00:04:17

**Key points**

- Flagship cadence ramped 1 → 3 → 4 events per year, stabilizing at ~4–5 flagships rather than over-stretching the core crew (`00:04:17`).
- Geographic expansion relies on local partnerships—following Shanghai with newly announced AI Engineer India (`00:04:17`).
- Community organizers can apply online to bring the format to their own city (`00:04:17`).
