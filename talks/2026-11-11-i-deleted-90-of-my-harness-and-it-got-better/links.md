## Resources

- **Blog** · [How Foundational Models Became Superhuman in Bash](https://www.philschmid.de/superhuman-bash)
  (philschmid.de, 2026-09-01). His write-up of the same experiment: rebuild the
  harness, strip every tool but Bash and a media viewer, compare score, tokens and
  cost.
- **Blog** · [The Bitter Lesson](http://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html)
  (Rich Sutton). The essay the talk opens with: general methods that use
  computation win, so the harness should shrink as models improve.
- **Product** · [Gemini Decisions API](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/decisions-api)
  (launched 2026-11-11, the morning of the talk). Typed yes/no, choice and score
  answers over text, images, audio, video and PDFs, with up to 1,000 questions per
  request.
- **Product** · [Gemini 4 Carbon](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-4-carbon)
  (generally available 2026-11-10). The model in the bash-only demos. It chains
  2.2 to 3.7 commands per bash call, where Flash chains 1.3 to 1.8.
- **Repo** · [earendil-works/pi](https://github.com/earendil-works/pi). Pi, the
  minimal coding harness used for the demos (transcribed "PyHarness"). Its default
  read/write/edit/bash tools are what the talk compares against bash only.
