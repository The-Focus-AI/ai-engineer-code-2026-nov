## Resources

- **Product** · [Asynchronous Authorization](https://auth0.com/ai/docs/intro/asynchronous-authorization)
  (Auth0 docs). CIBA: the agent asks for consent by push, SMS or email outside
  the browser session, as in the cancel-booking demo.
- **Product** · [Token Vault](https://auth0.com/docs/secure/call-apis-on-users-behalf/token-vault)
  (Auth0 docs). Auth0 holds the connected account's tokens so the agent gets a
  scoped, short-lived one: the GitHub connection in the Party Animals demo.
- **Blog** · [Auth0 for AI Agents is Now Generally Available](https://auth0.com/blog/auth0-for-ai-agents-generally-available/)
  (Auth0, 2025-11-19). The product the demos run on.
- **Blog** · [Your AI Agent Doesn't Need Your Card Number](https://www.focusotter.com/articles/your-ai-agent-doesnt-need-your-card-number)
  (Focus Otter, 2026-07-28). His write-up of the payment section's three options:
  the card itself, a capped virtual card, or a Stripe Shared Payment Token.
- **Product** · [MPP](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/machine/mpp) (Stripe docs).
  Stripe's Machine Payments Protocol, the HTTP 402 flow behind the payment demo.
- **Product** · [x402](https://www.x402.org/). Coinbase's HTTP 402 protocol, the
  other side of the talk's comparison.
