The Software Factory That Needs Zero FDEs

Raz Berman Gold, Overcut

Overcut argues that an enterprise software factory should not need forward-deployed engineers: it plugs into the tools and agents you already run, composes specialized governed agents into SDLC workflows with human gates, and improves itself through scored retrospective memories.
