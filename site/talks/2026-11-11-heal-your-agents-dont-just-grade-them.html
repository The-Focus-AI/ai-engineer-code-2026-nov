Heal Your Agents, Don't Just Grade Them

Khasimul Asif Mohammad, Splunk · Sudhir Tonse, Splunk

Evaluating agents is not enough: like an immune system, an agent platform should continuously detect failures across all sessions, diagnose them against a taxonomy, prescribe a fix, and remember it so the failure does not recur.
