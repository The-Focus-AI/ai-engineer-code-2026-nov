12-Factor-Factories: Design Patterns for Agentic Coding

Dexter Horthy, HumanLayer

Effective agentic software factories are built on timeless engineering practices—balancing automated forward pressure and rigorous back pressure, planning according to expected pain, and preserving human judgment—rather than unreviewed output. Because generation cost is approaching zero while review cost stays constant, teams must actively fight compounding AI slop and retain ownership over system understanding and verification.
