Post-Training Gemini 4 for Real Engineering Work

Melvin Johnson, Google DeepMind

Post-training Gemini 4 (Carbon and Hydrogen) for multi-day software engineering requires continuously sourcing non-saturated hard tasks, replacing binary test checks with rubric-based and sandbox-hardened grading that penalizes cheating, grounding completion claims in trajectory actions to reduce fabrication, and feeding failures from internal dogfooding at Google into rapid weekly training iterations.
