Brownfield Factories

Justin McCarthy, Diffusion

Software factories fail when applied to open-ended greenfield discovery, but excel on brownfield systems where existing customers and runtime signals already define the promises to maintain continuously in the background. Once a brownfield factory simplifies over time and runs autonomously, teams must build a companion demand factory to expand within their brand envelope rather than spilling excess software.
