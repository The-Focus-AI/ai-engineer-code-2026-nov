I deleted 90% of my harness (And It Got Better)

Philipp Schmid, Google DeepMind

Each model generation absorbs tools the last harness had to provide, so rebuild the harness from scratch every release: delete what the model now does itself, often down to one bash tool, and keep only what it can't do through a computer.
